Foveacast

Predicted attention heatmaps, right in your browser.

Free. No account. Nothing leaves your machine.

What this does

  • Predicts where a typical viewer will look first on your screenshot.
  • Runs the model in your browser — your image never leaves this tab.
  • Works on PNG or JPEG, up to 20 MB, desktop Chrome or Firefox.

It's a prediction, not an eye-tracking study. Useful for a sanity check before publish.

Your heatmap will appear here once you drop a screenshot.
