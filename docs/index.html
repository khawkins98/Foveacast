Drop or paste a screenshot (Cmd-V / Ctrl-V) to generate a predicted attention heatmap. PNG or JPEG, up to 20 MB. Controls appear after the first analysis.

Runs locally — your image never leaves this tab.

Heatmap outputs reflect population-average gaze patterns from the model's training data. They are estimates, not measurements of any specific person's attention.
