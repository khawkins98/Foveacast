# Foveacast

Foveacast takes a screenshot of a web page and shows you where people are likely to look. It runs in the browser, on your own machine. No account, no upload, no server, nothing leaves the device.

It's for comms staff, web officers, and designers who want a quick attention check on a layout without paying for a commercial predictive eye-tracking service.

**Status:** in development. Not usable yet.
